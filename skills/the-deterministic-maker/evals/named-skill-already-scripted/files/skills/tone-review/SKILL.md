# Tone Review

Release notes are read by people who are about to do work because of them. They want to know what changed and what to do, said plainly and without spin.

## Workflow

1. Read the whole draft once before judging any sentence. Tone is a property of the piece.
2. Decide who the reader is (operator, developer, end user) from the content. If you can't tell, ask.
3. Look for places where the tone works against that reader:
   - blame or defensiveness about a bug
   - excitement that hides a breaking change
   - hedging that leaves the reader unsure whether they must act
4. For each place, quote the sentence, say what a reader would feel, and offer one rewrite that keeps the facts.
5. Finish with one paragraph on the draft as a whole: what to keep, and the one change that would help most.

Don't score the draft. A number hides the reasons.
